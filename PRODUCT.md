# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Streamers who broadcast with OBS Studio on Windows, mostly Spanish-speaking, many of them small creators on TikTok and Twitch (also YouTube and Kick). They are not technical: the app has to explain itself. While live they glance at a narrow OBS dock (320–450 px wide) between gameplay or talking to camera; before going live they configure widgets in a normal desktop browser. Their viewers see the on-stream overlays (alerts, goals, top-gifter ranking) without ever touching the app.

## Product Purpose
Chorusly unifies the chat and events of Twitch, TikTok, YouTube and Kick in one OBS dock, and renders alerts, goals and a top-gifters ranking as OBS browser sources. It runs locally (Node server on port 8787, portable Chorusly.exe), free and open source. Success: a streamer installs it, connects in two steps and never misses a message, gift or moderation warning while live.

## Positioning
One local app for all four platforms at once, with TikTok as a first-class citizen (gifts, likes with floating hearts, shares, TikTok moderation warnings shown big), events that chat cannot fake, and no account or cloud service in between.

## Brand commitments
- App (dock, widgets page, icons, installer): direction «Pila» chosen by the owner — dark sober greys (#0e0f12 / #15161a / #26272e), one violet accent #8b7cff, Inter, everything straight and aligned, no tilts or ornaments. Logo: two stacked violet speech bubbles.
- On-stream overlays keep the «Pegatina Chorusly» sticker theme (pink #fe2c55, cyan #25f4ee, Bricolage Grotesque) by the owner's choice.
- The owner rejected a 3D cyan/red split effect on lettering and a harsh applause sound.
- All copy in correct Spanish, short and plain; no colloquial abbreviations.
- Never innerHTML with chat text; the server only answers this PC.

## Accessibility
The dock is read at arm's length in OBS: legibility and contrast over decoration. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
