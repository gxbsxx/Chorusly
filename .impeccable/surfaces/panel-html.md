# Chorusly: dock, widgets page and overlays

Scope: the whole app's look — OBS chat dock (panel.html, Operate, 320–450 px, glanced at while live), widgets configuration page (widgets.html, Operate, desktop browser), on-stream overlays (overlay.html: alerts, goals, ranking; seen by viewers). Function, copy and behaviour stay; the look is replaced around the confirmed brand (logo K3, ink/pink/cyan, Bricolage).

Audience and job: Spanish-speaking OBS streamers, non-technical. Read chat at a glance, never miss a real gift or moderation warning; configure widgets before going live. Viewers see the overlays.

Grounded candidates (ordered by resonance): 1 comic speech bubbles (tebeo); 2 backstage pass and festival tickets; 3 die-cut sticker sheet (pegatinas); 4 Spanish TV lower-thirds (rótulos); 5 football stands and tifos; 6 neon ON AIR sign; 7 photobooth strip. ## Direction contract
THESIS: Everything real that happens on stream arrives as a die-cut sticker slapped onto a calm ink sheet; plain chat stays plain text. Refuses the category default of a dark panel with glowing neon cards.
OWN-WORLD: Ink ground #15132a with a slightly lighter ink sheet; sticker paper cream #f2efe9 keyline around every sticker; sticker inks pink #fe2c55 (gifts, primary), cyan #25f4ee (follows, room, TikTok), yellow #ffd23f (subs, highlights), violet #a78bfa (Twitch bits, redeems). Bricolage Grotesque 700–800 on stickers and headings, Segoe UI/system for chat. Stickers: 2–3 px cream keyline, rounded die-cut corners, soft offset shadow, tiny rotation.
STORY: The streamer sees chat first; anything that matters (gift, follow, sub, warning) stands out as a sticker that cannot be faked by typing; viewers see the same stickers land on stream.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Dock: slim ink header (logo sticker, platform chips, gear), three tool buttons as flat pills, the event strip as a row of small stickers, the room as round face stickers, then full-height chat. Widgets page: same header, tabs, settings cards on the left, a preview canvas on the right with the stickers landing.
FORM: die-cut sticker sheet (pegatinas), candidate 3 of 7. seed:b6d1a5fd48a5d316. Signature move: the "slap" — a sticker lands with a short overshoot and settles with a 1–3° tilt and its shadow tightening, in the dock strip and in the on-stream alert.
